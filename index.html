Critical findings

Fix in Phase 1 (weeks 1–4) unless noted.

Critical

1 · 36 pages tell Google and AI engines not to quote them

Robots meta nosnippet, noarchive on 36 pages, including the hubs /dentist-in-dubai/, /cosmetic-dentistry-in-dubai/, /aesthetic-treatments-in-dubai/, /anti-aging-treatments-in-dubai/, /dermatology-skin-care-in-dubai/, plus all 17 root-level treatment shells.

Fix: remove it in Rank Math (page-type default and per-page Advanced tab). This takes minutes and is the biggest single AEO unlock.

Critical

2 · Lumos is absent from every non-brand AI / search answer tested

0 of 10 patient-style queries (e.g. "best dental clinic in Dubai", "veneers Dubai price", "Profhilo Dubai price") surfaced Lumos. Answers cite Doctify, Zavis, Property Finder, Bayut, Grazia and the pricing pages of King's Aesthetics and Emirates Hospitals. Brand queries return look-alikes (Lumara, Lumus, a Malaysian "Lumos").

Fix: GEO workstream: entity clean-up, directory citations, priced treatment answers. See docs/ai-visibility-baseline.md.

Critical

3 · GPTBot is rate-limited at the CDN (HTTP 429)

Re-tested 25 Oct. GPTBot gets 429 on every request; OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Googlebot and Bingbot get 200.

Fix: allow-list verified AI crawlers in Hostinger's CDN / bot-protection settings.

Critical

4 · Legacy "Fattan Polyclinic" listings conflict with Lumos at the same address (new)

Unit 3501, Aspin Tower is still listed as Fattan Dental & Derma / Fattan Polyclinic LLC on the DHR Waffer directory, Doctify, Edarabia and DRFive. Four phone numbers are in circulation (04 352 4140, 04 355 0236, 056 997 8196, 04 229 0018) and three sets of opening hours. Whether Lumos succeeded Fattan is inferred, not confirmed.

Fix: confirm the relationship with the clinic, then claim and rename or report each listing. NAP consistency is a primary Map Pack and AI-entity signal.

Critical

5 · Mobile performance 29/100, LCP 10.0 s, 14.4 MB homepage

Two autoplay hero videos are 7.6 MB on their own. Responsive images could save a further 2.9 MB. Server response is 1.8–2.1 s on every page because full-page caching is off.

Fix: poster image with tap-to-play video, enable LiteSpeed / hPanel caching, prune Elementor add-ons. Targets are in proposal §11.

Critical

6 · The core hub pages have no unique content; the dental hub shows lorem ipsum and "0 AED" (new)

The <main> element of /aesthetic-treatments-in-dubai/ and /cosmetic-dentistry-in-dubai/ is empty; all visible text is shared header and footer. /dentist-in-dubai/ shows lorem-ipsum copy and three "Starting at 0 AED" prices, which is a DHA advertising risk as well as an SEO one.

Fix: remove placeholders now; write hub copy in Phase 3 with real "from" prices.

Critical

7 · 16 treatments published twice; 6 more overlapping pages

Root-level shells (712 words, nosnippet, no H1) duplicate full /treatments/ pages with identical titles, e.g. /orthodontics/ and /treatments/orthodontics/. Overlaps include two root-canal pages, two pigmentation pages, four exosomes pages, /dental-treatments-dubai-2/ and /terms-and-conditions/ (2 words).

Fix: 27-rule 301 map ready in implementation/redirects.csv (Rank Math import). Validate against Search Console before applying.

High-priority findings

Phase 1–2.

High

8 · 57 pages have zero or multiple H1s

34 have none; 18 have 19 H1s, 4 have 15, and 1 has 3. The treatment template tags "Benefits", "Step 1" and related-treatment cards as H1. One template fix corrects most of them.

High

9 · No medical-entity schema; entity name inconsistent

Rank Math outputs Organization named "Lumos Polyclinic llc.", WebSite named "Lumos Dental & Aesthetic Clinic in Dubai", and Service. There is no MedicalClinic, Dentist, Physician, FAQPage, address, geo or hours. sameAs lists Facebook only. A draft graph is in implementation/schema-medicalclinic.jsonld.

High

10 · 29 pages have no meta description; 16 duplicate titles

Most of the duplicate titles are the root/treatments pairs; consolidation resolves them.

High

11 · Malformed slugs contain the domain name

/treatments/https-lumosclinics-com-tooth-extraction-in-dubai/, …-tmj-surgery-in-dubai/, …-skin-boosters-hydration-quality-in-dubai/. The last one is actually a wrinkle-treatment page.

High

12 · Sitemap includes utility pages and omits /about/

/my-account/, /customer-dashboard/, /latepoint/, /latepoint-booking/, /survey-page/, /sitemap/ and /locations.kml are listed. /about/ (doctor team) is live but missing. /contact/ and /contact-us/ return 404.

High

13 · Demo-theme content is still live (new)

/shine-by-lumos/ lists template posts ("App developers that are going to slow down in 2020"). Demo category archives (/mindset/, /news/, /cases/, /trend/, /style/…) are linked site-wide. They are noindexed, but they show to patients and waste internal links.

High

14 · No doctor credentials or profile pages (E-E-A-T)

Doctors are named without qualifications or DHA licence numbers. Competitors cited by AI run 13–55 doctor pages each.

High

15 · No informational content; no prices

0 blog articles. Competitors win "cost" queries with AED price pages (Emirates Hospitals, Zavis, Meddental) and 100–380-post blogs (Versailles, Dr. Michael's).

High

16 · Measurement is not in place

GTM is on all 127 pages, but a Search Console verification tag is on only 1 page and no conversion baseline exists. Ahrefs and Semrush data are unavailable on current plans, so rankings and backlinks are not yet measured.

Where to start

The first five need only WordPress and Hostinger access and no new content. Full checklist: docs/phase-1-plan.md.

  1. Remove nosnippet from all 36 pages in Rank Math.
  2. Allow-list GPTBot in Hostinger's CDN / bot protection.
  3. Remove lorem ipsum, "0 AED" prices and the review-link placeholder, and delete the demo-theme posts.
  4. Turn on full-page caching and replace the two hero videos with a poster image.
  5. Clean the sitemap: noindex utility pages, add /about/.
  6. Grant Google access (Search Console, GA4, Business Profile) so rankings, conversions and Map Pack baselines can be set, then begin the Fattan / NAP clean-up.

Method & limitations

All 128 sitemap URLs were fetched on 25 Oct 2026 and parsed with a Python crawler (title, meta, robots, canonical, headings, schema, images, links, body text). Counts are measured, not estimated. Raw data: audit/crawl-2026-10-25.csv. Performance: Lighthouse 12.2.1 run locally against headless Chromium (the PageSpeed API quota was exhausted). These are lab figures for single runs; no field (CrUX) data was returned. AI visibility: 13 patient-style web searches on a US-based index, used as a proxy for the sources AI assistants cite. Fetching the site with each AI crawler's user agent tested only user-agent handling; it does not verify crawler IP addresses. Not yet covered: keyword rankings and backlinks (Ahrefs plan does not include API access; Semrush has no API units), Search Console, GA4 and Google Business Profile data (access pending), and live prompt testing in ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity (Month 1 panel).
